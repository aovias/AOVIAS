# Customer Success Metrics

## Leading indicators (weekly)

| Metric | Target (pilot) | Source |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| WAUs / licensed seats | ≥ 40% by week 4 | Identity + seats |
| Evidence uploads / week | Agreed baseline | Compliance |
| % OEs with owner | ≥ 95% in-scope | OE matrix |
| Heartbeat freshness | < 7 days | Licensing |
| Open S1 | 0 | Support |

## Lagging indicators (monthly / quarterly)

| Metric | Target | Source |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Time to M1 | ≤ 7 days | CS notes |
| Time to M2 | ≤ 30 days | CS notes |
| CAPA > 30d trend | Down | CAPA aging |
| NRRish renewal intent | Verbal commit T−30 | CSM |
| Case studies approved | Pipeline ≥ 1 / quarter | Marketing |

## Health score (simple)

Start at 100; subtract:

- 25 Expired license  
- 15 Grace without renewal plan  
- 15 No evidence in 14 days  
- 10 Open S1  
- 10 Sponsor missed last review  
- 5 Training incomplete for QM/Admin  

| Score | Band |
|-------|------|
| 80–100 | Green |
| 50–79 | Yellow |
| < 50 | Red — escalate |

## Reporting

Roll into health reviews and QBRs. Do not invent product telemetry that does not exist — prefer customer-health API + manual attested metrics.
