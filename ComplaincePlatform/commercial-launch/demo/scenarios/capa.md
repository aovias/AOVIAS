# Scenario — CAPA from Deficiency

**Duration:** ~8 minutes  
**Persona:** Quality Manager  
**Entitlements:** `core.compliance` (CAPA domain; keys may also list `core.capa`)

## Story

Findings become managed CAPAs with ownership and aging—not email threads.

## Steps

1. Open Deficiency Command Center / findings list.  
2. Select a seeded deficiency or finding.  
3. Show promotion / CAPA creation path (or open an existing seeded CAPA).  
4. Walk status, owner, due date, and RCA fields if present.  
5. Tie back to the originating journey/standard when linked.

## Say this

“Accreditation pain is rarely ‘no checklist’—it is open issues that never close. CAPA is first-class.”

## Avoid

- Promising automatic RCA writing without AI entitlement  
- Deleting seeded CAPAs mid-demo  

## Success signal

Prospect mentions their current CAPA tool sprawl.
