# Scenario — Quality Manager Day

**Duration:** ~10 minutes  
**Persona:** Quality Manager  
**Entitlements:** `core.compliance` (minimum)

## Story

Show that a QM starts the day in a purpose-built home—not a blank admin console.

## Steps

1. Sign in as the demo QM user.  
2. Open the Quality Manager role home / dashboard.  
3. Point out journey progress, overdue tasks, and deficiency/CAPA signals.  
4. Drill into one in-progress journey; show department ownership.  
5. Open an evidence item; explain lock behavior after Submitted (do not force a bad edit).  
6. Navigate to My Tasks; complete or demonstrate one low-risk task if safe on staging.

## Say this

“Ownership is explicit. Status is not a free-text field—it moves through the lifecycle service.”

## Avoid

- Creating new production-like PHI  
- Long configuration detours (save for technical deep-dive)

## Success signal

Prospect nods that this replaces their morning spreadsheet triage.
