# Scenario — AI Assistant

**Duration:** ~8 minutes  
**Persona:** Quality Manager or Consultant  
**Entitlements:** `ai.assist` (optional `ace.insights`)

## Story

AI helps draft and navigate—humans approve. Never “the AI said we’re compliant.”

## Steps

1. Confirm license includes `ai.assist` before opening the panel.  
2. Open the grounded assistant in QM context.  
3. Ask a safe question, e.g. “What evidence is still missing for the open journey?” (use seeded non-PHI data).  
4. Show evidence reviewer cue if available on a sample evidence item.  
5. If Enterprise: open ACE insights surface briefly for readiness signal—label as insight, not verdict.  
6. Explicitly show or describe an approval/human gate for any draft artifact.

## Say this

“Advisory only. Entitlement-gated. No patient identifiers in prompts.”

## If AI is down

Skip gracefully: explain entitlement model and show published AI guide; continue to risk scenario.

## Success signal

Security/QM asks how to disable AI—answer: remove entitlements / policy, not only hide UI.
