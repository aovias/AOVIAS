# Scenario — Management Review

**Duration:** ~8 minutes  
**Persona:** Hospital Owner + Quality Manager  
**Entitlements:** `governance.risk` (committees / MR); Enterprise insights optional

## Story

Close the demo at leadership altitude: decisions from evidence, CAPA aging, and risk—not anecdotes.

## Steps

1. Open Committees / Management Review package (seeded).  
2. Walk agenda inputs: compliance posture, open CAPAs, top risks, incidents summary.  
3. If `ace.insights` present, show one insight tile as an input—not a decision.  
4. Switch to Hospital Owner home for portfolio visibility.  
5. End with commercial ask: package, seats, accreditation date → proposal.

## Say this

“Management review is where AOVIAS earns executive sponsorship. The system prepares the pack; leadership still decides.”

## Close line

“Next step is a scoped proposal—no credit-card checkout in v1.0.0—and an implementation timeline aimed at your assessment window.”

## Success signal

Executive names a sponsor and asks for the one-pager.
