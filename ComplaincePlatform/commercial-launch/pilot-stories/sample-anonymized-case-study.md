## Challenge

Quality tracked accreditation evidence across shared drives and Excel. Department ownership was unclear; CAPA aging was invisible to leadership until external audit week. Management review packs took days to assemble.

## Solution

AOVIAS Command deployed with Compliance Journey as lifecycle SSOT, OE ownership matrix, deficiency-based CAPA, audit sessions, and role homes for Owner / QM / Department Heads. AI assist enabled for grounded Q&A with citations (advisory only).

## Lessons learned

- Activate one framework version and freeze scope for the pilot.
- Train Department Heads before go-live or evidence stalls.
- Treat AI answers as assistive; require citations and human approval for accreditation claims.
