# Training Schedule (Pilot)

**Product:** AOVIAS Command v1.0.0  
**Duration:** Typically 2 weeks around go-live (adjust for hospital size)

## Week −1 (pre go-live)

| Day | Session | Audience | Duration | Outcomes |
|-----|---------|----------|----------|----------|
| D−5 | Admin workshop | ORG_ADMIN, IT | 2h | Setup, licensing, seats, backup awareness |
| D−4 | QM program workshop | Quality Manager | 3h | Framework, journey, ownership, scoring |
| D−3 | Department head clinic | Dept Heads (cohort) | 2h | Evidence upload, tasks, CAPA basics |
| D−2 | Auditor / consultant overview | External / internal auditors | 1.5h | Read paths, findings → CAPA |
| D−1 | Office hours | All trained roles | 1h | Open Q&A; capture blockers |

## Week 0 (go-live week)

| Day | Session | Audience | Duration | Outcomes |
|-----|---------|----------|----------|----------|
| D0 | Go-live huddle | QM + Admin + CS | 30m | Confirm M1 checklist |
| D2 | AI assist clinic (if entitled) | QM + power users | 1h | Citations, when not to trust AI |
| D5 | Mid-week triage | QM + Support | 45m | Open tickets; diagnostics practice |

## Week +1

| Day | Session | Audience | Duration | Outcomes |
|-----|---------|----------|----------|----------|
| D8 | CAPA & audit deep-dive | QM + Dept Heads | 2h | Deficiency CAPA SSOT, audit close gates |
| D10 | Management review / risk | Owner + QM | 1.5h | Dashboards, risk register, MR prep |
| D12 | Health review prep | CS + QM | 1h | Metrics for 30-day health review |

Record attendance in CS notes. Missed critical sessions (Admin / QM) block go-live until makeup is complete.
