# Content governance

Real framework **source rights** are **NOT VERIFIED**.

Formal **permitted-use evidence** for accreditation source content is **not recorded** in this repository. **NABH** has been contacted; **formal information is awaited**. Contact does not verify rights.

## Blocked until evidence is recorded

Do not ingest, extract, index, process with AI, paraphrase, or display **real** accreditation source content until source-specific permitted-use evidence has been reviewed and recorded in the canonical governance register.

## Current rules

- Do not add NABH standards text, objective elements, checklists, scores, or paraphrased normative content.
- Do not add other accreditation-body content unless a human records verified rights and points to evidence.
- Do not copy content from AOVIAS Command.
- A prototype must use **synthetic fixtures only** within the approved prototype boundary. Synthetic data does **not** establish production retrieval quality or source rights.

## Candidate frameworks

Cross-framework discovery scope and candidate frameworks (including NABH, JCI, NABL) are defined in canonical product documentation (**D1-01**). Candidate listing does **not** clear source rights.

## What a future record must show

A content-governance decision (`DEC`) or Confluence record needs:

- who holds the rights
- what use is permitted
- how provenance is shown
- how withdrawal works
