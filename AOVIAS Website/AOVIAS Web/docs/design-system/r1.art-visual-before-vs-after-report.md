# R1.ART — Visual Before vs After Report

**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Status:** Complete

---

## Before

- Heroes present but atmosphere felt thin
- Mid-page company/product sections often flat (`bg-muted/20` or plain canvas)
- Cards read as uniform white slabs
- Illustrations low-contrast; frames understated
- Large screens under-used illustration real estate

## After

- Rich blueprint atmosphere with depth layers and institutional marks
- Alternating feature / muted / blueprint / elevated bands
- Premium cards with gradient surfaces and accent hairlines
- Stronger diagram ink + elevated shells
- Larger hero/product visuals and XL composition balance

---

## Qualitative verdict

The site no longer reads as a single flat plane. It communicates engineering excellence through atmosphere, rhythm, and diagram storytelling while remaining calm and trustworthy.
