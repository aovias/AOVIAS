# R1.ART — Illustration System Report

**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Status:** Complete

---

## Family

All public illustrations remain in the AOVIAS SVG brand family under `apps/website/src/components/brand/`.

### Chrome (`BrandShell`)

- Elevated card (`shadow-raised`, `rounded-xl`)
- Soft gradient fill
- Domain-accent top hairline
- Ink inherits `--domain-accent`

### Ink ladder (`brandInk`)

Stroke/fill opacities increased for clearer readability in light and dark themes without neon or 3D effects.

### Atmosphere (`BrandAtmosphere`)

Densities: `subtle` | `default` | `rich`

Layers: institutional wash, blueprint grid, sparse engineering diagonals, primary radial light, restrained domain wash, corner marks, architectural seam.

---

## Principle

Illustrations teach structure (platform layers, workflows, trust) — they do not decorate emptily.
