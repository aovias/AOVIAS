# Phase V9 — Commerce Experience Report

## Objective

Transform AOVIAS commercial surfaces into an **enterprise procurement experience** — partnership-led evaluation and engagement, not consumer SaaS pricing or checkout.

## Surfaces

| Journey stage | Route | Atmosphere intent |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing / commercial evaluation | `/pricing` | Commercial Horizon (`enterprise-abstract`) |
| Checkout / procurement review | `/checkout` (+ billing, confirm, success) | Procurement |
| Enterprise inquiry + demo + contact sales | `/company/contact` (+ hashes) | Engagement / Collaboration / Partnership |

## Shared editorial rhythm (`CommerceEditorialTemplate`)

1. Commercial hero — partnership statement + illustration + CTA  
2. Pricing philosophy — transparency, scalability, flexibility, partnership  
3. Plan summaries — Evaluate / Professional / Enterprise (audience, deployment, support, governance, engagement)  
4. Interactive commerce band — published packaging (catalog-driven)  
5. Enterprise comparison matrix — procurement dimensions  
6. Commercial illustrations — trust-oriented DomainScenes  
7. Partnership journey — Discovery → Long-term partnership  
8. Closing CTA — consultation, not pressure  

Contact hub adds specialized inquiry paths and a Demo Experience section.

## Messaging principles

- Partnership over transactions  
- Capability over feature lists  
- Outcomes over promotional pricing  
- Long-term value over short-term cost  
- No “Most Popular” badges, flash discounts, or countdown tactics  

## Outcome

Procurement teams, CIOs, and technical evaluators can move from philosophy → comparison → consultation with a calm, enterprise-grade tone.
