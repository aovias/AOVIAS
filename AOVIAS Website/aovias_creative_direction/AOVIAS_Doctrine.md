---

## 1. Identity

AOVIAS is an institutional technology company. The visual language must communicate trust, responsibility, engineering excellence, clarity, confidence, stability, and long-term thinking.

We are not a startup brand. We do not chase novelty, neon, or decorative spectacle.

## 2. Metaphor

**Bauhaus-era engineering journal, reimagined for modern enterprise.**

Precision line work. Archival calm. Modern signal accents.

## 3. Hierarchy of expression

1. Typography  
2. Composition and whitespace  
3. Atmosphere (named, reusable)  
4. Illustration and diagram  
5. Color accent (one per viewport)

## 4. Color doctrine

- Neutrals and void/parchment surfaces carry the brand.  
- Signal blue (`#4A6FA5` intent) is the singular interactive accent.  
- Product and domain accents are restrained markers — never page washes.  
- **One accent per viewport.**

## 5. Atmosphere doctrine

Backgrounds are a **system**, not page decoration. Every major surface declares a named atmosphere (Enterprise, Aurora, Blueprint, Workflow, Architecture, Knowledge, Security, AI, Platform, Corporate, Minimal).

Atmospheres support content; they never compete with it.

## 6. Illustration doctrine

- Single stroke weight (1.25px)  
- Isometric lock at 30° for product/platform art  
- Outlines only — no gradient fills in line illustrations  
- Platinum structure lines at ~60% opacity  
- Exactly one accent highlight per illustration  

## 7. Hero doctrine

Asymmetric composition: calm copy field + dominant visual.  
Hero budget: brand signal, one headline, one sentence, CTA group, one visual.  
No floating badges, promo chips, or stat strips on hero media.

## 8. Motion doctrine

Motion explains, guides, confirms, orients, reveals, or connects.  
Signature moments: Horizon Reveal, Node Focus, Audit Trail Live, Silent Confirmation.  
No bounce, elastic, or entertainment motion.

## 9. Navigation doctrine

Navigation is enterprise application chrome — elevated, precise, keyboard-aware — not brochure ornament.

## 10. Governance

Future visual work references the **AOVIAS Visual Language Specification (AVLS)**.  
Creative frames inspire; AVLS specifies; the Design System implements.
