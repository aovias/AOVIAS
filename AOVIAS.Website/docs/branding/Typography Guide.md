# AOVIAS Typography Guide (Brand)

## Wordmark

- All-caps geometric sans  
- Open “A” forms (no crossbar) matching the mark’s chevron language  
- Tracking slightly open for institutional calm  

UI product typography remains governed by the **UI Design System** (`packages/design-system`). This guide covers brand lockups and marketing titles on social/presentation assets.

## Social / presentation titles

- Short, one line preferred on OG images  
- Sentence case for descriptions; product names retain official casing (`AOVIAS Command`)  
- Avoid all-caps paragraphs  

## Do not

- Substitute playful display fonts for the wordmark  
- Fake the wordmark with generic Inter/Roboto stacks in logo files — use the SVG master
