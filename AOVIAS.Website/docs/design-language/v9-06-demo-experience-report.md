# Phase V9 — Demo Experience Report

## Route

`/company/contact#form-demo` (+ `#demo-experience`)

## Positioning

A demo request starts a **consulting engagement**, not a marketing webinar.

## Transparency

| Step | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Confirmation | Acknowledge request; confirm product & stakeholders |
| Response timing | Typical scheduling outreach within two business days |
| Discovery | Short conversation on environment, constraints, goals |
| Demonstration | Focused technical walkthrough |
| Follow-up | Clear next steps |

## Implementation

`DemoExperienceSection` — PrincipleCards + `TechnologyStoryFlow` on the Contact page, ahead of forms.
