# T3 — AI Capability Matrix

**Date:** 2026-07-20

---

Capabilities describe **outcomes**, not algorithms. None promise autonomous decision-making for critical institutional work.

| Capability | Outcome | Human role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intelligent recommendations | Surface relevant options | Choose and own the decision |
| Workflow assistance | Reduce friction in routine steps | Approve consequential steps |
| Knowledge discovery | Find policies and guidance faster | Interpret and apply |
| Compliance guidance | Point toward applicable rules | Interpret and act |
| Operational insights | Highlight what deserves attention | Prioritize and respond |
| Pattern recognition | Detect recurring signals | Validate and escalate |
| Predictive alerts | Notify of emerging conditions | Review — not auto-resolve |
| Natural language assistance | Draft, search, clarify | Review and control |

## Explicit non-claims

- No replacement of professional or institutional judgment  
- No autonomous conclusion of critical outcomes  
- No model/vendor/infrastructure disclosure
