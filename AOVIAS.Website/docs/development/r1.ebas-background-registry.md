## Theme behaviour

- **Light:** patterns use `--color-border` and `--domain-accent` over `--surface-hero` / `--background`.
- **Dark:** same definitions; tokens shift to navy charcoal surfaces and brighter accent steps.
- No separate hex overrides — theme fidelity is automatic.
