# T3 — AI Principles Summary

**Date:** 2026-07-20

---

## Intent

Present enduring commitments — not a feature list — for how AOVIAS designs and applies intelligence.

## Principles

1. **Human-centered design** — shaped around operators and duty of care  
2. **Transparency** — users understand when assistance is present  
3. **Explainability** — understanding over opaque novelty  
4. **Accountability** — people and institutions remain accountable  
5. **Privacy** — sensitive information treated with restraint  
6. **Security** — secure-by-design expectations apply  
7. **Fairness** — biased outcomes treated as an ongoing concern  
8. **Reliability** — dependable enough for institutional use  
9. **Continuous improvement** — practice improves; principles endure  

## Editorial note

Tone remains calm and engineering-led. No vendor language, no hype, no “AI-first” positioning.
