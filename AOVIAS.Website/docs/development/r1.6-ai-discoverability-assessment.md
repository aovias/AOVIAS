## Guidance for AI-facing content

1. Prefer entity registry names in copy and metadata.
2. Keep one H1 per page; nest H2/H3 by section purpose.
3. Link products ↔ technology ↔ company with descriptive anchors.
4. Do not add invisible text, cloaking, or schema that is not on-page.
