# R1.ART — Enterprise Art Direction Report

**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Sprint:** Enterprise Art Direction & Visual Storytelling  
**Status:** Complete

---

## Direction

AOVIAS should feel like a mature institutional technology company: calm, engineered, memorable — peer-level with Microsoft / IBM / Stripe / Linear maturity, without copying their surfaces.

### Do

- Blueprint grids, architectural seams, soft radial light
- Layered depth via surfaces + elevated illustration frames
- Intentional brand color on accents, diagrams, markers, CTAs
- One strong hero composition per page (no carousels)

### Don’t

- Startup neon, glassmorphism, decorative blobs, artificial 3D
- Flooding pages with color
- Redesigning IA, routing, or component APIs

---

## Visual system levers (this sprint)

| Lever | Change |
| --- | --- |
