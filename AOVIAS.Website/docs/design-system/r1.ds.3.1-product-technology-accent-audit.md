**Status:** Complete

## Mechanism

`resolveDomainAccentAttrs(pathname)` → `DomainAccentScope` sets `data-product` / `data-technology` / `data-industry` → CSS maps to `--domain-accent`.

Accents appear on eyebrows, icons (`IconWrapper` tone `domain`), badges, and brand diagrams (`text-domain-accent`). Pages are never full-tint recolored.
