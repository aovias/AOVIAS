# User Guide — AOVIAS Command v1.0.0

**Audience:** Quality managers, department heads, consultants, auditors

## Getting started

1. Sign in with your hospital identity.
2. Open your role home (Owner / QM / Department Head / Consultant / Auditor).
3. Confirm your assigned organization and hospital context.

## Core workflows

### Compliance journeys

- Open a framework journey to see lifecycle status (Draft → In Progress → Submitted → …).
- Only authorized roles can transition journeys; invalid transitions are rejected by the state machine.
- Add or update evidence while the journey is unlocked. After **Submitted**, evidence is locked.

### Tasks and assignments

- Work from My Tasks / department queues.
- Complete evidence requests and reviews; escalate blocked items to the Quality Manager.

### CAPA and deficiencies

- Deficiencies appear in the command center; promote findings to CAPA when remediation is required.
- Capture RCA where policy demands; track actions to closure.

### Audit prep

- Use the auditor workspace / audit packages to assemble evidence and journey status for visits.
- Prefer exported packages over ad-hoc file shares.

### Governance (if licensed)

With `governance.risk`: maintain risk register entries, incidents, controlled documents, and committee/MR items from governance modules.

### AI assistance (if licensed)

With `ai.assist` / `ace.insights`: ask the assistant for grounded help, review evidence cues, and view readiness insights. Always treat AI output as advisory—human approval remains mandatory.

## Productivity tips

- Keep correlation-friendly support tickets: include timestamp, screen, and organization name.
- Never paste patient-identifiable data into AI prompts or support emails.
- Use search and role dashboards before creating duplicate CAPAs or risks.
