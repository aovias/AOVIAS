# AOVIAS Command — Sales Deck (Outline)

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Use:** Discovery + first meeting (20–30 min)

## Slide 1 — Brand

**AOVIAS Command**  
Enterprise healthcare compliance for accreditation-ready hospitals.

## Slide 2 — The problem

- Accreditation prep living in spreadsheets and email
- Evidence that cannot prove who changed what, when
- CAPA aging invisible to leadership
- AI tools that invent answers auditors will reject

## Slide 3 — The promise

One platform: compliance journeys, evidence discipline, CAPA, governance, optional grounded AI—licensed by entitlement.

## Slide 4 — Who buys / who uses

| Buyer | User |
|-------|------|
| Hospital Owner / CEO | Portfolio readiness |
| Quality Director / QM | Day-to-day program |
| CIO | Deploy, license, security |

## Slide 5 — Core story (5 minutes)

Journey SSOT → department ownership → evidence lock on submit → deficiency → CAPA → audit package.

## Slide 6 — Governance story

Risk register, incidents/RCA, document control, management review (`governance.risk`).

## Slide 7 — AI story

`ai.assist` + `ace.insights`: advisory, grounded, entitlement-gated. Human remains accountable.

## Slide 8 — Commercial packages

Command Core · + AI Assist · Governance · Enterprise (see pricing one-pager).

## Slide 9 — Trust

Multi-tenant, licensing heartbeats, security headers, Key Vault option, SBOM/checksums, correlation IDs.

## Slide 10 — Proof path

Guided demo (demo runbook) → pilot scope → implementation timeline → proposal.

## Slide 11 — Ask

Schedule technical deep-dive + request bed count / accreditation date for quote.

## Speaker notes

Stay honest: no self-serve payments in v1.0.0; no fabricated customer logos until signed pilots.
