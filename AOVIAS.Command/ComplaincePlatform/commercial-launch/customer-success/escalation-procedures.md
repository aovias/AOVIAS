# CS Escalation Procedures

## When CS escalates (relationship / outcome)

| Trigger | Escalate to | Parallel |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Health score Red | CS lead | Support L2 if technical |
| Sponsor unreachable 14d | CS lead + Sales | — |
| Renewal at risk < T−30 | CS lead + Sales director | — |
| Customer threatens churn / legal | Exec sponsor | Legal |
| Suspected security / privacy incident | Security on-call | Support S1 |
| Data loss / wrong-tenant suspicion | Eng director | Support S1 |

## Severity vs Support

CS escalations are **not** a substitute for Support severity. Open a Support ticket for technical S1/S2 even if CS is already engaged.

## Internal bridge call (30m)

Agenda:

1. Customer impact statement (CSM)  
2. Technical status (Support/Eng)  
3. Commercial constraints (Sales)  
4. Single customer-facing message owner  
5. Next update time  

## Customer exec note (template)

> [Name], we are treating [issue] as a top priority. [Owner] is coordinating technical recovery; I own business communication. Next update by [time]. Ticket [ID].

## Post-incident

- Update health score  
- Add QBR retrospective item  
- Consider service credit **only** via commercial policy (not product automation)
