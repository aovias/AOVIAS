# Scenario — Risk Register

**Duration:** ~8 minutes  
**Persona:** Quality Manager / Patient Safety  
**Entitlements:** `governance.risk`

## Story

Risk-based thinking lives beside compliance—not in a separate forgotten spreadsheet.

## Steps

1. Open Governance → Risk Register.  
2. Open a seeded risk; show inherent/residual scoring fields as implemented.  
3. Link conversation to a related incident or control if present in seed.  
4. Explain how residual risk feeds management review later.  
5. Mention document control adjacency without deep-diving QDMS unless asked.

## Say this

“Governance entitlement unlocks the QMS spine: risk, incidents, documents, committees.”

## If entitlement missing

Show pricing SKU table path: Command Governance / Enterprise; do not hack data.

## Success signal

Prospect connects risk register to their infection control or safety committee.
